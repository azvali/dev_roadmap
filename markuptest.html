Detroit Auto Show Returns to January After Five-Year Experiment

By Marcus Webb, Automotive Correspondent

January 14, 2026

The North American International Auto Show will return to its traditional January slot next year, organizers announced Tuesday, ending a five-year run of summer dates that failed to draw the crowds the city had hoped for. Attendance during the June experiment averaged 400,000 visitors, down sharply from the 800,000 the show routinely pulled in its January heyday. Organizers cited competition from summer festivals and unpredictable outdoor programming as key factors.

A spokesperson framed the reversal as a return to form:

"January is when this city shows the world what it can build. We're coming home."
